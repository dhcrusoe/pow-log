    3 · What happened
    

    

  


  

    This page holds no canonicalisation logic of its own. It sends your draft to
    https://api.proofofworth.org/v0/check, which returns the exact bytes to
    sign and a template with a slot for the signature. The page signs, substitutes, and
    posts. That is the whole client — and it means this page cannot drift out of
    agreement with the network about what a record is.
