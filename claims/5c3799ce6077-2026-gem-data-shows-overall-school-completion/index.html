# 2026 UNESCO GEM Report Education Digest

## What’s New in 2026
- **Completion rates are up:** Globally, more students now complete upper secondary education than ever before, with progress noted in most regions.
- **Out-of-school rates remain stubborn:** Despite surging completion rates, many children are still not enrolled, especially in marginalized and hard-to-reach communities.
- **Equity gaps persist:** National averages often mask dramatic regional, gender, or economic disparities—some groups still lag far behind their peers in both access and completion.
- **Policy levers matter:** Differences in progress are related to country-specific policies, school demand, and institutional factors.

## Why it Matters
Sustained improvement in school completion is essential for economic mobility and social inclusion, but reporting must not ignore those still left out.
